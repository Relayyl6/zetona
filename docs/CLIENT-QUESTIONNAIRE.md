## Photo preparation guide (send with the message)

- **How many:** Start with 20-40 of your best photos. Quality beats quantity, and your strongest work leads.
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG or WebP. Export at full size (long edge at least 2400 px). Don't add logos or borders.
- **Variety for the home page:** Pick at least 8 photos for the home collage: a mix of tall portraits, squares, and a couple of wide shots, with plenty of visual interest.
- **Consent:** Make sure the people in your photos agree to have them shown online.
- **Descriptions:** For each photo, think of one short sentence describing it (this helps people using screen readers and helps Google find your work). Example: "Portrait of a woman in a white shirt against a deep blue studio backdrop."
- **Cover photos:** Choose one standout photo for each collection.
